PulseBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version

PulseBoard is a free Windows app made by Pulseforge. PulseBoard does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything it measures stays on your PC.

Data that stays on your PC

  • System information: CPU, memory, GPU, disk, network and battery readings are shown live and are not sent anywhere.
  • Screen time: The names of the apps you use and how long you use them are saved only on your PC, in the app's own data folder. You can delete this history at any time in the app, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Settings and backgrounds: Your preferences and any background picture you choose are saved in the same folder on your PC.

When PulseBoard connects to the internet

  • Speed test: Test data is exchanged with Cloudflare's speed test servers (speed.cloudflare.com). To show your public IP address, internet provider and server location, the app also contacts ipinfo.io. This happens only when you start a test.
  • Network Monitor: When you turn it on, the app sends ping requests to the server you choose (for example 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8).
  • Updates: The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store. The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for new versions.
  • Report a problem: If you choose to report a problem, a GitHub page opens in your browser with your app version and basic system details filled in. Nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself.

These services have their own privacy policies. PulseBoard does not send them any information about you beyond what is needed for the request.

No ads, no tracking

PulseBoard has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

PulseBoard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/nabinla/PulseBoard/issues.
